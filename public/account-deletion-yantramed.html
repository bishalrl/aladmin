Delete your YantraMed account

This page is for the mobile app YantraMed, published by Sikau Paisa. Use it to request permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

  1. Open https://aladmin.sikaupaisa.com/delete-account/yantramed (this page).
  2. Enter the email address you use to sign in to YantraMed.
  3. Optionally add a reason, then submit the deletion request on the form at that URL.
  4. We process valid requests and aim to complete deletion within 30 days.
  5. To cancel a pending request, email hello.sikaupaisa@gmail.com before deletion is completed.

Prefer the interactive form? Open the full deletion request page.

Data that is deleted

  • Account profile (email, display name, auth identifiers)
  • App progress and usage data stored for YantraMed
  • Subscription / entitlement records tied to your account in our systems
  • Support messages linked to your account (where stored by us)

Data that may be kept

  • Billing records required for tax, accounting, fraud prevention, or payment-provider obligations (e.g. Paddle invoices)
  • Security / audit logs for a limited period to detect abuse
  • Data in backups until those backups expire on their normal schedule

Retention period

We aim to complete account and personal-data deletion within 30 days of a valid request. Some billing or legal records may be retained longer where required by law.
